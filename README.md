# Pedro Sampaio de Camargo

**Currently:** Developing hardware and edge computing architectures for autonomous robots at ISR-UC.  
**Ask me about:** SIEM detection and investigation, and ROS 2.

Engineering student at the **University of Coimbra** (FCTUC, DEEC, Polo II), collaborating with the **Human-Centered Mobile Robotics Lab (HCMR)** at **ISR-UC** (Institute of Systems and Robotics).

From managing high-risk military and cybersecurity environments, I pivoted to foundational tech development. Today, at the University of Coimbra (ISR), I build the hardware and edge computing architectures powering autonomous robots and AI.

Driven by first-principles thinking, my goal is to advance human-machine integration. I build to elevate human capacity and am always looking to connect with others pushing the absolute limits of robotics.

I'm easygoing but fiercely dedicated when it counts. I value deep, practical execution over industry buzzwords.

## Cybersecurity: The "Assume Breach" Years (2023–2024)

### **Citadel Cyber Security** — *Rehovot, Israel*
**SOC Analyst (Tier 1→3 + Consultant) | Multi-tenant MSSP defending 40+ critical infrastructure clients**

| Domain | What I Did |
|--------|------------|
| **SIEM Engineering** | Operated **5+ SIEMs simultaneously** (Splunk, Sentinel, QRadar, CrowdStrike). Wrote **SPL/KQL** detection logic daily. |
| **Threat Hunting** | Proactive hunting via behavioral paradoxes. Built lookup tables & automated correlation funnels. |
| **Email Perimeter** | Raw SMTP header forensics, sandbox detonation, lookalike domain takedowns via SPF/DKIM/DMARC enforcement. |
| **Endpoint Containment** | Remote EDR shell forensics. **Owner from alert → investigation → client report → remediation rule.** |
| **CTI Integration** | Daily parsing of intel feeds → dynamic watchlist injection with false-positive stress testing against 30-day logs. |
| **CrowdStrike Outage** | Live incident (19/07/2024): Recovery across fleet when the sensor *became* the threat. |

> **Philosophy forged there:** MTTD and MTTC metrics: Dwell time in seconds, not days. The attacker is already inside — our job is to make their stay expensive and short.
